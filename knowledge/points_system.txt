# FORMULA 1 POINTS SYSTEM AND CHAMPIONSHIPS

## Overview
Formula 1 awards championship points to drivers and constructors based on their finishing positions in Grand Prix races and Sprint races throughout the season.

## Grand Prix Race Points (Full Race Distance)
Points are awarded to the top 10 finishers in every full Grand Prix:
- 1st Place (Winner): 25 points
- 2nd Place: 18 points
- 3rd Place: 15 points
- 4th Place: 12 points
- 5th Place: 10 points
- 6th Place: 8 points
- 7th Place: 6 points
- 8th Place: 4 points
- 9th Place: 2 points
- 10th Place: 1 point
- Positions 11 to 20: 0 points

## Fastest Lap Bonus Point
- 1 bonus point is awarded to the driver who sets the fastest official lap of the Grand Prix, PROVIDED that driver finishes in the top 10 (positions 1st through 10th).
- If the driver with fastest lap finishes in 11th or lower (or DNFs), no fastest lap point is awarded to anyone for that race.

## Sprint Race Points
Points are awarded to the top 8 finishers in the 100 km Sprint race:
- 1st Place: 8 points
- 2nd Place: 7 points
- 3rd Place: 6 points
- 4th Place: 5 points
- 5th Place: 4 points
- 6th Place: 3 points
- 7th Place: 2 points
- 8th Place: 1 point

## Shortened Races (Sliding Scale after Spa 2021)
If a race is stopped and cannot be resumed:
1. Less than 25% distance completed: Points awarded to top 5 (6, 4, 3, 2, 1).
2. 25% to 50% distance completed: Points awarded to top 9 (13, 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1).
3. 50% to 75% distance completed: Points awarded to top 10 (19, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1).
4. Over 75% distance completed: Full 100% points awarded.

## Championships
- World Drivers' Championship (WDC): Total individual points accumulated across the entire season by a driver. The highest total wins the world championship.
- World Constructors' Championship (WCC): Sum of points scored by both cars / drivers of each constructor (team) across the season. Prize money allocation is distributed according to final WCC standings.
